Help and support

Updated: 17 November 2026

Local work and an optional online workspace. You can keep a profile, selected PDF CV and saved opportunities on your phone. Online account, provider and application features depend on the service and its configuration. The hosted service is a development preview. A sign-in method or employer integration described here is available only when enabled; saving a job or profile does not authorize sending an application.

Get started in three steps

Open the Career Finder app on your phone. Its five sections are Search, Saved, Apply, Profile and Settings. Local profile and saved-opportunity tools do not require an online workspace.

  1. Find a job. Search in the phone app, or save a job link you already have. You can browse without signing in.
  2. Prepare your profile. Add your contact details and choose your PDF CV. Review your information before saving it online.
  3. Choose how to apply. Open the employer’s form, or review an application for Auto Apply when an authorized integration is available.
How does job search work?

Choose up to four public employer boards, add a role or keywords, and optionally add a location or select remote roles. You can add a public Greenhouse or Lever employer-board address.

Search covers the boards you select. Check the original posting for requirements and location restrictions, including for remote jobs.

If a source cannot refresh, the app shows when any retained results were last checked. A source error is different from finding no matching jobs. If results change while you load another page, start a new search.

How do I keep track after applying?

Open a job in Saved and choose Edit application record. Add a stage, applied date, follow-up date, or private note. The Follow-ups filter lists open records in date order. These details stay on the phone and do not create an employer-confirmed receipt.

Removing a bookmark keeps its application record. Closing an application keeps the applied mark. To correct an applied mark, choose Saved and confirm Mark as not applied; this does not withdraw an employer-held application.

Can I add the web app to my home screen?

On iPhone, open Career Finder in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app if offered. An internet connection is needed to open this web app and confirm an account. Clearing browser data can remove its local drafts.

Sign in and connect your phone

Using the web app? Choose Sign in from the header or Settings, then create your private workspace from Profile. You do not need a connection code. Save on this phone keeps a draft locally; Save profile online uploads the details and CV you selected.

Using the native iPhone or Android app? Follow these connection steps:

  1. Open Connect and choose an available sign-in method: Google, LinkedIn, or ChatGPT. A method becomes available only after its provider setup is complete.
  2. Create a connection code. Open the connection settings in the phone app and enter the code.
  3. Review your phone draft, then choose what to save online. Connecting alone does not upload your CV.

A code works once and expires after five minutes. Use the same account when you connect another phone to recover your saved information.

Can I sign in with Google or LinkedIn?

When enabled, the Google and LinkedIn buttons sign you into Career Finder directly through that provider. ChatGPT remains a separate sign-in option and may also offer Google on its own sign-in screen. To keep the same workspace, use your original sign-in method or sign in first and connect another method from Account. Matching email addresses never merge accounts automatically. Career Finder does not receive your provider passwords.

Why must my application email match?

Auto Apply uses the email of the account you connected. This helps prevent someone from submitting under another person’s email. You can keep a different email in a local draft and use an employer’s own form.

My code expired or my phone is lost

Generate a new code if the old one expired. For a lost phone, open Account and disconnect it, then connect your replacement. Disconnecting stops future account access; it cannot erase files already on a device.

Use your LinkedIn profile

Add your personal LinkedIn profile URL in the phone app’s profile form. It is optional and separate from your portfolio. The app removes tracking information from the link.

Choose Search LinkedIn to open LinkedIn with your search keywords and location. Finish any LinkedIn sign-in or application on LinkedIn.

What does saving a LinkedIn link do?

It saves the profile link you provide. It does not connect or verify your LinkedIn account, import your employment history, or allow Career Finder to apply through LinkedIn. You will see the exact link in an application review before an authorized employer integration can receive it.

Can I change or remove the link?

Yes. Edit or clear the LinkedIn field and save your profile. Prepare a new application review after any change. Earlier application records keep the information you approved at the time.

Understand your application status

Ready for approval
Check the employer, profile, CV, links, and answers. Nothing is sent until you approve.
Confirmed receipt
The supported provider returned an application ID. This confirms receipt, not recruiter review or selection.
Uncertain
We could not confirm receipt. Check your email or the employer’s application portal before trying again. Auto Apply blocks a resend to avoid duplicates.
Rate limited
The provider asked you to wait. After the displayed waiting period, reopen the review and approve a retry.
Manual application
Use the employer’s application page. Saving a job or marking it applied elsewhere does not create a verified receipt.
Why is Auto Apply unavailable?

An employer must authorize its integration and provide current application requirements. Jobs with unsupported questions use the employer’s own form. A saved profile or LinkedIn link does not enable an employer integration.

Should I keep the app open?

Yes, while approved applications are processing. Closing the app can leave unsent work waiting until you return. A request that has already started may finish after you close the app. Stop prevents remaining unsent applications; it cannot recall a request already sent.

Keep control of your data

Open Account to download your records or CVs, disconnect phones, or delete your cloud workspace. Read the privacy notice for details.

My CV upload stopped

Keep your PDF on your phone. After one minute, open Account and choose Recover interrupted uploads. A recovered file stays private. Save the profile from your phone to attach it, or upload the original PDF again if asked.

Another phone changed my profile

Review both versions in the phone app before choosing which to keep. Your unsaved phone draft stays available.

Another browser tab changed my phone draft

The web app keeps the newer saved version and asks you to reload. Keep the current page open if it contains unsaved edits. Export a recovery copy before resetting damaged or unfamiliar saved data. A different account or a newly created workspace never silently adopts an earlier account’s draft.

What happens when I delete my workspace?

The service removes active account records and CV files. It cannot recall applications sent to employers or erase local files on a phone. Deletion also removes duplicate-prevention history, so keep your export and check earlier applications before starting again.

What are the preview limits?

A workspace can keep five PDFs within 25 MiB, 200 opportunities, 100 application reviews, 500 application records, and ten phone connections. Each PDF can be up to 5 MiB. The service currently allows up to 250 workspaces and has shared storage and application limits. A capacity message preserves existing records and does not start a paid upgrade. Choose English, Arabic, or your device language in Settings. Arabic uses a right-to-left layout. Job postings, names, CVs, and text you write stay in their original language.

Still need help?

Report a reproducible technical problem in the project’s public issue tracker. Include your phone model, OS and app versions, and the steps that caused the problem.

Keep private information out of public issues. Do not attach your CV, email address, connection code, session token, or an unredacted account export. This is a development preview; a response time is not guaranteed.

These public pages and support requests

These help and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and can be read without signing into Career Finder. They add no sign-in form, analytics script, advertising, or embedded external media. GitHub receives a browser request when you visit and states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without signing in. Read GitHub Pages data collection information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The Career Finder issue tracker is public. Posting requires a GitHub account, and your username, request and attachments can be seen by the maintainer and other people. Content may remain public or be copied by others. Do not include your CV, email address, connection code, session token, private application notes or an unredacted export. This channel does not guarantee a response time, confidentiality or removal. Use GitHub’s available editing and privacy controls for your public posts.

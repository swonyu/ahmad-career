# Product and review research — useful improvements without crowded screens

**Research checkpoint: 17 November 2026. This is a product decision brief, not a competitor performance test or a release acceptance receipt.**

The strongest direction for Career Finder is a dependable, portable application workspace. Improve the existing Saved search, make existing records useful outside the app, and let a person export a reminder for a date they already chose. The evidence supports reliable organization and control. It does not establish that copying an AI score, bulk application feature or larger dashboard improves hiring outcomes.

This brief covers **Teal, Huntr, Simplify, Jobscan, LinkedIn, Indeed, Bayt, Naukrigulf and Saudi Jadarat**. It records 30 primary source pages and 24 selected public user review excerpts. The selection is deliberately qualitative and includes positive and critical experiences. The [source/review ledger](strict-review-evidence/continuation-2026-11-17-cloud/product-research/source-ledger.json) preserves URLs, retrieval mode, dates, limitations and the exact selected review identities. The integration owner owns the separate open-source comparison and any resulting implementation. The recommendations below are proposed follow-up work until an implementation receipt says otherwise.

**Development scope:** Saved-note search, CSV and calendar-file exports are hosted-web follow-ups. Native saved-note search code has also been written, but compilation and CI verification are pending. CSV and calendar-file additions are currently hosted-web only. None of this changes hosted version 7 or establishes a store release. The new disclosures have not had rendered browser acceptance, and completed file saves and calendar imports remain unverified; the implementation receipt must preserve those limits.

## 1. Research method and limits

Official help, product, pricing and app-store descriptions establish what a provider says it offers. Individual review text on Trustpilot, G2 and Google Play supplies reported experience. Platform-generated AI summaries, seller testimonials and competitor-written affiliate/SEO comparisons were not used as independent user evidence. No competitor account was created, subscription bought, extension installed, private content accessed or application submitted. Only the free built-in public search/retrieval tools were used; paid Parallel research was not used.

Review selection is not random. Public pages choose which reviews appear, and language, country, device and crawl snapshot can change the visible sample. A complaint is a useful scenario to test, not proof of the current product's defect rate or the provider's intent. Some indexed review excerpts disappeared from the later visible page; the ledger identifies those as indexed excerpts rather than pretending the whole current review corpus was downloaded. Repeated themes across different products support design priorities, not population-wide prevalence estimates.

Most selected experiences were published in 2026. One LinkedIn review is from August 2025 and is explicitly historical. Small Trustpilot samples matter: the retrieved Huntr and Simplify profiles each contained 22 reviews. These profiles also warned that reviews may not be representative. G2's Jobscan sample contained 14 reviews and labeled individual reviewer verification/source; neither label establishes that a claimed interview or hiring result was caused by the tool. Star ratings are therefore not used as a product league table. [Huntr reviews][R02] [Simplify reviews][R03] [Jobscan G2][R05]

Access gaps are retained. The direct Chrome Web Store review view for Simplify was unavailable. Several individual Trustpilot permalink requests were restricted, although their text was visible on the public company review pages. Indeed's help article was available through indexed official text but its current redirect could not be opened. A Jadarat consultation decision linked by HRDF returned HTTP 403; its content is unknown here. These limitations were not bypassed.

### Jadarat: evidence exists, but it is a different kind

No comparable accessible independent individual-review sample was established for Jadarat in this pass. HRDF's official application FAQ explicitly covers unavailable application data, unexplained eligibility refusal, qualification verification and CV replacement. These are documented support scenarios, not a measured frequency of failures. Its PDF instruction currently describes a 2 MB upload limit; Career Finder's own 5 MiB validation does not promise compliance with that external service. [Application help][O17]

HRDF's **Q3 2025** customer-voice report gives Jadarat individuals 71% satisfaction and establishments 85%. Those figures are historical, provider-published survey results and are not comparable to app-store stars or a 2026 result. The retrieval did not establish a complete Jadarat-specific sampling frame, so no confidence interval or trend claim is made. [Historical survey][O20]

A separate HRDF notice dated **5 Nov 2026**, updated **10 Nov**, says a decision followed a Jadarat public consultation. The linked decision was access-denied. We can report that the notice exists; we cannot claim which changes it approved. General HRDF portal usefulness percentages were excluded because they measure other pages or the broader portal, not the Jadarat application experience. [Consultation notice][O19]

## 4. Recurring needs and their implication

**Organization earns its place.** Positive Teal, Huntr and Simplify reviews name notes, manageable stages and having the relevant information together. This supports keeping Career Finder's present Search/Saved/Apply/Profile/Settings structure. It does not create a case for separate networking, analytics, task-board or AI dashboards. The feature inventory already contains stages, notes and follow-up dates; adding a second record manager would duplicate value. [R01] [R02] [R03]

**Interruptions are more consequential than missing decoration.** Independent reports across Indeed and Naukrigulf describe the file-picker or external-application path failing after effort has already been invested. Bayt and Naukrigulf reports add blocked profile-entry scenarios. Career Finder's demonstrated PDF/recovery fixes address the same class of need, but competitor reviews are not evidence that Career Finder's physical-device acceptance has passed. Keep its existing validation and recovery gates. [R07] [R08] [R09] [R10]

**Assistance must preserve user intent.** Reports about incorrect autofill and Jobscan score uncertainty reinforce an inspectable boundary. Simplify's own help distinguishes filling, saving and actual submission; Jobscan distinguishes its score from the employer's ATS output. Career Finder already records reviewed payloads and separates personal application marks from service evidence. Future convenience features must preserve that distinction. [R03] [R05] [O05] [O10]

**Portability and low-noise follow-up are reasonable design inferences.** Teal documents exports, Simplify supports spreadsheet import, and LinkedIn/Indeed describe retention/display limits. An exportable personal record reduces dependency on any one board. The reminder recommendation also responds to follow-up utility and notification fatigue. These sources motivate a testable product hypothesis; they do not prove that CSV or calendar files improve employment outcomes. [O02] [O06] [O11] [O13] [R03] [R06]

**Arabic/Gulf support needs precise scope.** Naukrigulf supplies Arabic interaction and regional filtering, while Jadarat has a specific citizenship and identity boundary. A user's Arabic language, Saudi location and work eligibility are different facts. Keep source coverage, user-entered country, remote restrictions and external eligibility distinct. Do not equate a translated interface with access to every Gulf employer. [R10] [O16] [O18] [O07]

### 1 — Search that respects Arabic and private context

The benefit is immediate: a person remembers a recruiter detail or Arabic note and finds the existing job without changing sections. Normalize only the comparison text. Search must not rewrite the stored or displayed job title, note, user input or document. The raw JSON record retains source values; derived CSV/calendar views deliberately use their documented output escaping and, for formula-like CSV cells, a visible safety marker. Document the chosen Arabic equivalences; do not quietly translate, infer synonyms or collapse unrelated words. Searching must stay inside the currently authorized local scope and must not expose a locked or quarantined account's note.

Meaningful checks should cover a note-only match, Arabic vowel marks and tatweel, ordinary Latin case, mixed Arabic/Latin values, empty input, long bounded notes, and distinctions the chosen normalization must preserve. A failed or cleared search should not mutate records. Test a language change with a dirty editor and an account change with a search active. This is a local matching improvement; it does not broaden external job discovery or source coverage.

### 2 — CSV as another view of the existing record

A compact row can contain title, employer, canonical public job URL, user-entered stage, applied/follow-up dates, personal note and last-edited time. State whether the export includes all current private records or only saved bookmarks; silently dropping a retained record because its bookmark was removed would undermine portability. Label personal statuses as self-entered and keep service-confirmed receipts separately identifiable.

Do not include session credentials, provider tokens, private account identifiers, entire CV bytes, unrelated profile fields or quarantined records. Use the same current-identity checks as existing exports. Treat spreadsheet formula interpretation as an output concern: neutralize dangerous leading formula characters without changing stored notes, quote fields correctly, and document how the exported cell is made safe. Preserve Arabic, multiline text and canonical dates. Keep the existing fuller export available where CSV intentionally omits information.

Tests should parse the result independently and cover commas, quotes, CR/LF, Arabic, formula-like strings, empty fields, duplicate saved/record references, a record without a bookmark, and a delayed export after identity invalidation. A spreadsheet export is not an automatic re-import, sync service or account recovery credential.

### 3 — A deliberate calendar reminder

Use the already entered civil follow-up day. A date-only reminder should stay on that day across device time zones; do not invent a meeting time or convert it through local midnight UTC. The file should describe a personal follow-up, not an interview scheduled by an employer. Generate it locally, with no calendar account access, recipient, attendee, invitation, external write or automatic email.

Display a short explanation next to the contextual action: the person chooses whether to import the file, and the calendar's notification settings determine whether it alerts them. An exported file cannot silently stay synchronized when the Career Finder date later changes. Avoid putting the private note or sensitive profile content in the calendar description by default; a job title, employer and canonical public job URL are sufficient context. The action uses the saved record; an unfinished editor copy is not an export target.

The implementation review should cover valid/leap/end-of-year civil days, all-day DATE and duration semantics (or exclusive end dates if DTEND is used), stable/appropriate identifiers, escaping and line folding for Unicode, malicious line-break input, and identity invalidation before download. Test actual import into available calendar clients with synthetic data before claiming compatibility. The integration owner owns the standards detail and implementation decision.

## 6. What to preserve and what to defer

The current draft-preservation, confirmed-identity and application-receipt work remains foundational. The product research does not justify relaxing provider consent, verified-email policy, original-account recovery, file bounds, explicit approval or employer integration requirements. Local/calendar exports are useful precisely because they can fit without an external permission grant. Simplify's own privacy documentation illustrates that a browser autofill extension processes form/page content; a new extension would create a materially larger collection and maintenance surface than the three proposed local changes. [Privacy][O08]

Keep freeform personal history where a closed vocabulary is unnecessary. Retain clear retry states, the previous valid CV, saved job links and source timestamps. Application-record exports use the canonical public job identity, which removes known tracking parameters and normalizes the URL; they do not promise the exact originally pasted URL. Distinguish a failed source from a successful search with no matches. Preserve the current distinction between personal tracking and service dispatch. These are acceptance requirements for the existing product, not a request for extra explanatory cards on every screen.

Defer bulk application automation, a generic ATS percentage, a social feed, recruiter CRM, email inbox sync, a permanent calendar dashboard and multiple AI generators. The reviewed products demonstrate that these are separate, often paid workflows with significant handling and verification costs. The selected reviews establish no need to reproduce all of them in Career Finder. Its live Auto Apply gate remains closed without an authorized employer integration; no real test applications are permitted.

Likewise, Gulf links can be documented as external resources when relevant, but a link must not imply an official integration or eligibility decision. Do not add every reviewed service to the main navigation. A support/help reference or existing external-resource disclosure is sufficient if the owner chooses to add one later.

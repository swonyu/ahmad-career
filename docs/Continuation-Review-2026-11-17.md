### Two distinct account deletion fences

| Fence | Retained data and lifetime | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner lifecycle | A durable HMAC of the owner identity, opaque generation and deletion timestamp in `account_lifecycles`; no plaintext account ID, email, profile, résumé or credential | Reject a pre-deletion pairing, workspace-open or linking mutation even if it resumes much later or first reads lifecycle state after deletion. Deletion advances the generation atomically. A new deliberate request after deletion may create fresh data. This marker does **not** expire after ten minutes. |
| Provider callback | An HMAC of the provider subject, deletion timestamp and expiry in `auth_deletion_fences`; expires after the maximum pending OAuth transaction lifetime of **ten minutes** | Prevent a login begun before deletion from relinking the just-deleted provider identity while an old callback could still be valid. This is separate from the durable owner marker and does not replace it. |

Deleting an account removes its application and provider/session records as documented while retaining these narrowly scoped safety records for their stated lifetimes. The durable marker preserves no application content or reusable authority. Completed deletion must not be described as removing every infrastructure fence.

## Corrections and remaining unknowns

- **Replace Heavy's unnamed “official app listings / help (prior passes)” row** with these exact links and explicit per-feature boundaries. Do not carry an unsupported numerical saved-job/application cap or an unlimited allowance into the comparison.
- **Do not flatten Premium boundaries.** GulfTalent's broad free marketing coexists with explicit Premium insight features and job-page Easy Apply labels. Naukrigulf's free app listing coexists with limited-free/paid AI-tool terms. No paid service is recommended here.
- **Keep publisher claims separate from measurements.** No ranking, success rate, number of available jobs/employers, notification timeliness, performance advantage or universal reliability claim was adopted. No reviews or ratings were used as primary capability evidence.
- **Do not infer an integration.** A competitor's regional listings, employer messages or single-click application description does not supply Career Finder with an authorized feed, reusable-contact entity or cross-site autofill capability.
- **Hands-on gaps remain:** current free-account enforcement, exact saved-job and alert limits, cross-device continuity, offline recovery, alert permissions/delivery, real application outcomes, accessibility and Arabic task completion. These unknowns should not be labelled missing features.

Retrieval limitations: Bayt's old `/en/mobile-promo/` returned 410; Naukrigulf `/mobile-apps` returned 502 and its mobile homepage timed out. Those errors were bypassed with the exact official store listings and do not establish that the products are unavailable. Naukrigulf's [FAQ](https://www.naukrigulf.com/jobseeker/faq?locale=en) exposed the fee question but not its answer; its registration page exposed only the JavaScript-disabled notice. Neither was used to invent a free-application answer. The older Naukrigulf Android update page was not used as current acceptance evidence.

# Regional competitor critique — root verification follow-up

Access date: 18 November 2026. These are published capability checks, not hands-on competitor account tests. Root independently opened the sources after Grok Heavy supplied its correction.

## Accepted evidence and correction

- **Bayt application status:** the official [tracking guide](https://www.bayt.com/en/blog/32432/how-to-track-your-job-applications-on-bayt-com-like-a-pro/) describes a dashboard with application dates, CV/profile views and application status, including submission, employer viewing and shortlisting. This establishes an advertised status capability. It does not establish that every employer supplies updates, the exact free-tier depth, or actual Arabic UI behavior.
- **Bayt store claims:** the current [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bayt) describes filters, alerts, regional coverage and free core search/application/CV functions with optional Premium. Root did not find Grok's two quoted tracking phrases in that retrieved listing. They are not adopted as verified Play quotations; the official blog is the source for tracking.
- **Naukrigulf AI entitlement:** the official [terms page](https://www.naukrigulf.com/terms-and-conditions), in the CV Maker / Interview Q&As Pro / CV Analyser subsection, describes limited free functionality and paid additional functionality. Exact current quotas remain unknown. Do not infer that every AI tool is free, or transfer an iOS guest-application description to Android.
- **GulfTalent:** retain the earlier verified distinction between core advertised functions, Premium profile-viewer identity/applicant comparison, and Premium Easy Apply labels on two particular job pages. The labels do not demonstrate logged-in enforcement or eliminate possible alternative application routes.

The original Grok response and follow-up are preserved. Root sent these source-specific corrections back to the same Heavy session. Both implementation conversations remained in Build; the research conversation remained in Heavy. A displayed source-count badge is not a count of independently verified sources.

## Resulting acceptance decisions

1. Finish the already validated Career Finder dialog focus and data export journeys. A usable local stage filter, correct focus, unambiguous personal marks and readable exports are measurable improvements; they do not imply universal superiority.
2. Keep personal application marks distinct from employer-confirmed receipts. Competitor-advertised employer status cannot be reproduced by relabeling a local record.
3. Benchmark search relevance and freshness within disclosed source coverage. Broader catalog or provider work remains with its current owner.
4. Compare complete Arabic tasks, keyboard and physical assistive-technology behavior. A language declaration in a store listing is not that proof.
5. Maintain free/paid and platform-specific boundaries in the competitor matrix. Do not add unvalidated contact-reuse, autofill or AI scope solely to fill a feature checklist.

This follow-up changes evidence attribution and acceptance priorities; it authorizes no new package migration, native build, paid service, account, source upload or store release.

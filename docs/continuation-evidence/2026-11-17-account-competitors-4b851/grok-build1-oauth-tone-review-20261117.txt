ca3 hosted-auth.mjs already drives the real handler. I did not re-run it: Miniflare is not installed here, and I did not add a package. The existing check "mismatched sign-out fails and invalid-cookie recovery clears no Sites account" expects:

A live session plus a different expectedAccountId returns 409 and leaves browser_sessions at 1. That assertion does not inspect Set-Cookie.
An expired cookie plus expectedAccountId: "" returns 200, redirectTo /signout-with-chatgpt?return_to=%2Fconnect, and headers.get("set-cookie") matches Max-Age=0. auth_identities stays 1.

Those are response headers. headers.get("set-cookie") is not getSetCookie(), and neither is a browser cookie jar. On the success path the handler appends two clears: __Host-career-session and __Host-career-oauth, each Path=/; Secure; HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax; Max-Age=0.

The header-versus-jar race cannot be executed in this session. Protocol for a later real browser, without changing sign-out security:

One Chromium profile, loopback only, no live provider. Read HttpOnly cookies with the devtools cookie store, not document.cookie.
Hold the sign-out response. Abort before any bytes: the newer session cookie must remain, and the page must not navigate.
Release the two Set-Cookie clears, then abort during the body: record the jar separately from the header list. Applying Max-Age=0 after headers arrive is ordinary fetch timing, not a new account bypass. Account rows stay.
A still-valid session with the wrong expectedAccountId must stay 409 and must not send either clear.

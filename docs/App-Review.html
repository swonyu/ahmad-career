What was reviewed

The review covered the native SwiftUI iOS interface and store, the shared employer adapters, the macOS WebKit boundary, the website UI and local storage, the development server, the generated Worker, and the automated checks. It also examined the distinction between researched opportunities and new employer leads, package calculations, exports, input validation, accessibility and failure behavior.

The main weakness was at boundaries: old research versus new employer facts, a failed request versus a successful empty search, saved state versus a live feed, and an in-memory edit versus a durable save. The fixes make those distinctions explicit. The product remains a personal research and application-preparation workspace.

Unreadable saved records could be erased

Observed riskA malformed workspace could fall back to empty state and then be overwritten; malformed cached rows could also crash navigation.

05

Remaining product limits · not unresolved defects

What the app can support today

  • The researched shortlist is dated 8 October 2026.Ten roles were researched at that time. Seven are with Qiddiya, so this is a concentrated shortlist. It is not an exhaustive comparison of Saudi employers. Publication dates and future deadlines do not guarantee that a vacancy is still accepting applications.
  • Live search covers seven selected employer boards, with bounded samples.The selected sources are Alcon, Johnson & Johnson, Roche, Novartis, GSK, BD and Qiddiya. Title filtering, result limits and missing or multiple-location details can omit suitable roles. An absent result does not establish closure.
  • Live leads have not received the same review as the top ten.They need a personal check of degree, relevant years, location, seniority and role scope. Verified Saudi geography and a relevant title do not prove that Ahmad meets the requirements.
  • Salary is not verified for live leads.The original vacancies did not disclose salary in the research snapshot. Historical market guides are negotiation context, not an offer or a role-specific forecast. Basic salary, fixed allowances and discretionary bonus must remain separate.
  • The full CV and achievement evidence are missing.The public record supports medical-supply KPI supervision. Exact career dates, degree details, decision authority, team scope and measurable outcomes still need confirmation. University attendance must not be counted as employment.
  • There is no account or device synchronization.Website/browser, iPhone and Mac progress are local to their storage environment. Cross-window coordination does not provide cloud backup. Web Locks provide stronger simultaneous-tab protection where supported; older environments fall back to fresh reads and storage events.
  • The iOS package uses development signing.Installation requires a device registered in the provisioning profile. Physical phone installation remains unverified. Simulator or build success alone does not confirm that the package is installed and working on the phone.
  • Applications stay under the user’s control.The app prepares notes, opens official employer links and tracks manually entered status. It does not submit an application, send a message or turn a status change into an employer action.

Product critique

A clearer role for the app

Useful for deciding where to spend effort.The researched fit labels, qualification gaps and application steps support a focused shortlist. They should continue to be presented as reasoned, conditional judgments.

Useful for keeping a personal record.Independent saved/tracked snapshots and complete exports protect continuity when employer feeds change. Local storage and missing sync need to stay visible.

Useful for discovering new leads.The live feeds add current employer evidence. Their greatest value comes from honest coverage and failure reporting, rather than a claim to cover every Saudi vacancy.

Still dependent on verified personal evidence.A full CV and substantiated outcomes will improve the quality of any future matching. The app should continue to avoid invented credentials, salary promises or implied interview probabilities.

BETA DESCRIPTION
Ahmad Career is a career workspace focused on healthcare supply chain and
performance leadership opportunities in Saudi Arabia. Review a dated researched
shortlist, discover new leads from selected employer boards, save opportunities,
track application progress and prepare notes using your own career evidence.

Fit judgments are provisional. The app does not promise employment, verify user
credentials, submit applications or guarantee pay. Live search uses bounded
employer queries; it does not claim to cover all Saudi vacancies.

BETA REVIEW NOTES
No username, password, payment or subscription is required.
The Top 10 and saved records are available offline. Employer search requires
internet access and external boards may change or be unavailable. Previously
saved results remain available when an entire refresh fails.

The native iOS app is independent of the private companion website and GitHub
repository: reviewers do not need access to either. The bundled research uses
publicly available professional and employer information and identifies gaps.
User-entered profile records remain local until the user explicitly shares notes.
External employer links open in the browser. Users submit applications themselves.

The initial requested audience is Ahmad and a small beta group. Do not claim a
planned public App Store release unless the owner confirms that intention.

Privacy & beta support

Career Finder provides a local workspace for reviewing opportunities, preparing notes and tracking applications. You can enter your own professional details and planning preferences. Its current live sources provide limited Saudi-oriented samples; choosing another country or obtaining the app in another store does not expand that coverage. Running a live search contacts the configured recruitment services. Sharing a note sends a copy only to a destination you choose.

This page describes the native iPhone and iPad app. It does not describe the separate website or Mac app.

What stays in the app

Details you enter
Optional professional details, including experience, qualifications, tools, responsibilities and achievements, are saved locally when you save your profile. Country, city, role keywords, planning currency and period, display time zone, and discovery criteria are also local preferences. These details and preferences are not included in employer search requests. Professional details can appear in notes you choose to export; planning and discovery preferences are excluded.
Your job workspace
Saved roles, application statuses, completed preparation steps, retained vacancy details, the latest accepted search results and source-check information are stored locally. Per-role application dates and receipt notes are separate local records. These records let you continue after closing the app. Changing an application status does not submit an application or contact an employer.
Salary comparisons and list filters
The offer calculator processes the amounts and period you enter on the device. Calculator amounts are not saved to the workspace or sent to employer services. The selected planning currency and period are local profile preferences. No exchange-rate conversion is performed. Existing monthly SAR floors and dated SAR guide figures retain their original units and meaning. Text used to filter a displayed list is not sent to an employer.
Public research included with the app
The app retains dated employer-listing information only to help recover earlier saved or tracked records when their stored snapshot is missing. Existing snapshots take precedence. Fresh workspaces do not receive a personal biography or those old roles as current recommendations. Bundled reference material is separate from your entered details and is not a connection to your LinkedIn account.

This candidate does not load ads or include an advertising SDK. There is no app account, developer-operated cloud sync, tracking SDK or developer analytics service in this version. The app does not automatically upload your saved profile or application records to the developer. Apple’s TestFlight diagnostics are a separate data flow, explained below.

Employer searches and external websites

When you choose to search or refresh employer results, the current native app sends fixed job-search requests directly over HTTPS to six configured Workday recruitment sources: Alcon, Novartis, Johnson & Johnson, GSK, BD and Roche. It also retrieves details for selected vacancies. These are bounded samples focused on Saudi locations and selected role families; they are not a complete search of all jobs. Older Qiddiya records may remain in an existing workspace, but Qiddiya is not in the current refresh schedule.

Those requests contain predefined job queries, location filters, pagination information or vacancy identifiers. They do not contain your entered profile, country or role preferences, planning currency, time zone, local discovery criteria, saved-job selections, application statuses, receipt notes or preparation notes. The recruitment services receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address, request timing and HTTP request information. Their own practices govern any server logs they retain.

Opening an application, LinkedIn or salary-source link opens an external website. The website and browser may process information under their own privacy settings and policies. Any account, CV upload or application you submit there is managed by that provider. Career Finder does not submit job applications for you.

Notes you choose to share

A single-role preparation note includes the role, preparation guidance and application status; it excludes profile details and receipt notes. A full tracking export can include your professional details, preparation checklist, saved or tracked roles, application dates and receipt notes. When an unsaved receipt draft is included, it is labelled as a draft. Local planning and discovery preferences are excluded from both note types. Exports are readable notes, not an importable backup. The system share sheet lets you choose an app, person or storage destination.

Review the note before sending it. Once shared, the copy is handled by the destination you select. Later changes or deletion in Career Finder do not recall that copy. Avoid entering confidential employer information or details you do not want included in an export.

Keeping and removing local records

Your saved workspace has no automatic expiry. A successful refresh updates accepted results; saved or tracked vacancy snapshots may remain so that your application record survives a listing disappearing. If every employer check fails, previous accepted results are preserved. A partial refresh distinguishes successful and failed source observations. An employer response, an unavailable listing and your own application status are separate records.

  • You can edit or clear profile fields, remove a role from Saved, change its application status to Not started, and uncheck completed steps.
  • Removing a saved role or resetting its status does not necessarily erase every locally retained vacancy snapshot.
  • To remove the app’s local workspace, use Delete App in iPhone or iPad Settings. Offload App keeps its documents and data.
  • Device or computer backups may retain copies according to your backup settings. Restoring a backup can restore app records. Manage those backups separately.
  • Exports and feedback already sent elsewhere must be managed at their destination. Deleting the app does not remove them.

Dated employer reference material is part of the app itself. Clearing personal entries does not edit that bundled material. Reinstalling starts a fresh workspace unless an operating-system backup or another retained app-data mechanism restores earlier records.

Apple TestFlight is separate

If you install this beta through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the application provider. This can include device, app-version and testing information. Feedback you send can include your comments, screenshots and diagnostics. Identity information visible to the provider depends on how you were invited and what you submit.

These TestFlight processes are separate from the app’s local storage and employer searches. Review Apple’s TestFlight & Privacy notice for the information involved, Apple’s retention practices and available privacy controls.

Beta support and corrections

If you installed the app through TestFlight, open TestFlight → this app → Send Beta Feedback. Describe the screen, the steps you took and what happened. Check screenshots and attachments before sending them; they may show profile or application information.

You can also use beta feedback to flag inaccurate or outdated employer information. Do not include a CV or sensitive personal documents unless you intentionally choose to share them.

Career Finder keeps a phone workspace for opportunities, drafts and application records. Connecting the hosted service adds an account-backed application workspace: you explicitly save applicant details and a PDF CV, review each application and approve eligible submissions. Opening the app or connecting a phone does not upload its existing draft.

Current activation state: the hosted candidate has no employer credentials configured and live submission is disabled (liveEnabled: false). A configured service address, test receipt or successful app build does not establish that an employer received an application.

Information on your phone

The phone keeps your applicant draft, any locally saved selected PDF bytes, saved opportunity links and connection settings. The iPhone also retains its separate local planning profile, discovery preferences, saved roles, self-entered application records, preparation steps and accepted search results. Updating a tracking status or receipt note does not send an application or create a provider receipt.

The application profile starts empty. Existing local planning records are not silently converted into applications. A failed connection, expired token or hosted account deletion preserves the phone draft. Shared notes and receipt exports create separate copies at the destination you choose.

On iPhone, the device connection token is kept in Keychain and draft files use iOS file protection. Opening a browser setup, support or account link does not place the token or CV in that link.

Hosted account, recovery and saved data

The hosted flow opens connection setup in your browser. ChatGPT sign-in supplies the account identity and email needed to associate and recover the workspace. A one-use code authorizes a phone connection. Use the same signed-in account to recover that workspace or connect another phone. The applicant email used for hosted submissions must match the signed-in account email.

Explicitly saving to the connected service uploads the applicant fields you provide, such as name, contact details, location, professional information, preferences and website, plus the selected CV. Adding an opportunity or preparing an application sends the chosen job link and details, message and employer-specific data choices. The service retains reviews, approval records, submission attempts and provider receipts so an interrupted phone request cannot silently create a duplicate.

The hosted design uses Sites-managed Workers, a D1 database and private R2 CV storage. A CV must be a PDF of at most 5 MiB. Each account is limited to five stored CV files and 25 MiB of CV content. These are storage limits, not a promise of automatic deletion after a fixed number of days. HTTPS services also receive ordinary connection information, including IP address, request timing and HTTP information.

Custom application services use their own operator, account-recovery process and retention practices. The phone preserves an existing custom service address and identifies its operator-managed flow separately from the hosted Career Finder account.

What an approved application sends

When an authorized employer integration is activated, approval permits the service to send the reviewed applicant fields, PDF CV and explicit choices to the supported Lever posting. Unsupported jobs remain available through their employer website. Future-opportunity contact is a separate explicit choice; declining it is not inferred as consent.

A verified receipt requires affirmative provider acceptance and an application identifier. It records API acceptance, not recruiter review, an interview or a job offer. An uncertain outcome is not automatically retried. A provider rate-limit rejection requires a new visible approval after the cooldown before hosted processing continues.

The hosted phone flow continues only work already approved on the server. Closing the app pauses further continuation requests; a request already sent may still finish. Employer copies are subject to the employer's privacy policy and are not recalled by deleting the Career Finder workspace.

Retention, disconnection and deletion

Hosted account records and stored CV versions remain until account deletion or a supported control removes them, subject to the storage limits above. Selecting a new current CV does not itself promise erasure of older stored versions. Use Manage account and delete hosted data for the hosted deletion flow. Disconnecting a phone revokes that connection; it does not delete the hosted workspace.

Requesting deletion immediately fences new uploads. An application request already in flight can temporarily block completion of deletion while its outcome is resolved. The account page must show that pending or blocked state rather than report that deletion has finished.

Completed deletion removes account-linked records, CV content and identifying CV metadata. Empty, randomly named storage markers can remain to prevent a previously started upload from recreating a deleted file. These markers retain no account association, filename, CV hash or CV content.

Deletion does not erase employers' copies, exports, phone drafts or operating-system backups. Manage those separately. Removing the app's local documents differs from offloading an app while retaining its data. A custom service's operator controls deletion of data sent to that service.

Employer searches, websites and sharing

The iPhone's existing discovery feature sends fixed job queries directly to selected recruitment services over HTTPS. It currently checks bounded Saudi-oriented samples and does not establish coverage of other countries or every profession. These search requests exclude your applicant CV, locally entered profile, preferences, application notes and tracking records. The recruitment services receive ordinary network request information.

Opening an employer or other external link uses that website's own account, application and privacy practices. Review notes, screenshots and receipts before sharing them; exports can contain professional details and application information. Later edits or deletion do not recall copies already shared.

Diagnostics and feedback

The native candidate does not include advertising or tracking SDKs. Platform operations and support feedback are separate from applicant submissions. The publisher must disclose the actual operational logging and any retained support copies in the final published policy; this draft does not invent a retention period or a legal exception to deletion.

The developer's handling and retention of separately stored beta feedback must be confirmed before public use of this policy.

USING THE WORKSPACE
The dated 9 October research contains nine healthcare leads, without adding
unsuitable roles to reach ten. All nine have undisclosed salary. Live search
queries six healthcare employers: Alcon, Novartis, Johnson & Johnson, GSK, BD and
Roche. Qiddiya is not queried. Coverage is bounded; missing jobs do not prove closure.

1. Review each role's conditional fit, requirements and employer source.
2. Add qualifications and achievements you can substantiate in Profile.
3. Optionally set local discovery criteria: Riyadh, minimum monthly SAR and whether
   to show unverified pay. Defaults are any Saudi city, no pay floor and unknown
   pay shown. Unknown pay never counts as meeting a minimum. Hiding unverified pay
   with an active floor may empty discovery; saved/tracked roles remain available.
4. Save or track leads. Recheck location, grade, availability and pay with employers.
5. Review notes before sharing and submit applications personally.

There is no guarantee of qualification, director-level seniority or salary.
Historical salary guides are context, not vacancy-specific offers. No new employer
applications or employer messages were sent during this review.

PRIVACY AND EXISTING DATA
Public research uses public professional and employer information. Private CVs,
messages, photos and pay preferences are excluded from public source/packages.
Profile details, criteria, saved roles and tracking stay in each browser/app on
its device, without account sync. Criteria are excluded from search requests and
exported notes; notes may include professional details the user enters. Existing
saved data remains supported when optional criteria fields are absent. Clearing
browser data or removing an app can erase it. Exports are readable notes, not an
importable backup.

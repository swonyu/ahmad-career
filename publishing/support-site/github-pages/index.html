Help and privacy

Guidance for using Career Finder’s local phone features and an optional online workspace.

Find the information you need

  • Using Career Finder and getting support — profiles, existing PDF CVs, saved opportunities, connection choices and application status.
  • Privacy and your data — what stays local, what is sent when you choose online features, retention and deletion.

Keep public requests free of private information

The project issue tracker is public and requires GitHub sign-in to post. Describe a technical problem with a non-sensitive example. Do not attach your CV, account email, tokens, connection codes or an unredacted export. No response time or confidentiality is guaranteed.

Languages

These pages are available in English and Arabic. The links at the top switch this website’s language. In the phone app, choose your preferred interface language in Settings; your profile, CV and employer content remain in their original language.

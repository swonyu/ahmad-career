Privacy and your data

Updated: 17 November 2026

Local work and an optional online workspace. You can keep a profile, selected PDF CV and saved opportunities on your phone. Online account, provider and application features depend on the service and its configuration. The hosted service is a development preview. A sign-in method or employer integration described here is available only when enabled; saving a job or profile does not authorize sending an application.

What this notice covers

This notice covers the hosted Career Finder service and the iPhone and Android apps that connect to it. The service is a development preview maintained through the Career Finder project. The support page describes the available support route.

What stays on your phone

You can keep a local profile, selected CV, and saved opportunities on your phone. Connecting to the service does not automatically upload an existing phone draft. The app asks you to review or save the information you choose to sync. Local records can remain after you disconnect or delete a cloud workspace.

The phone web app keeps drafts, bookmarks, private notes, follow-up dates, and earlier search results in encrypted browser storage. Each guest or account workspace has a separate draft. The browser stores its encryption key as a non-exportable key; this protects the stored file format but does not protect information from someone who can use your unlocked browser or run code on this website. No personal information is cached by a service worker. An internet connection is needed to open the web app and confirm a signed-in account.

Unfinished browser profile and private-record edits are also kept in a separate encrypted recovery store for their original account and workspace. If storage is unavailable, the app explains that edits remain only on the open page and offers a separate export. Save on this phone keeps an unfinished profile locally. Save profile online uploads the profile and selected CV to your private workspace. Preparing an application uploads the selected job links. Application stages and private notes are not attached to employer requests. A browser data export contains readable personal information, including saved CVs and unfinished recovery copies when present. It is a readable record and cannot be automatically restored into the app, so keep the file private. Clearing browser data can remove local drafts permanently.

Searching public employer boards

When you choose Search in the phone app, it sends your chosen employer boards, keywords, location text, and remote-work filter to the public Career Finder discovery service. Browsing does not require an account and does not send your application token, profile, CV, email address, or application history.

The service retrieves public listings from the selected Greenhouse and Lever boards and filters them for your search. It does not forward your keywords or location filter to those employers. Public board data is cached for ten minutes; clearly labeled earlier results can be shown for up to 24 hours if a refresh fails. The application does not save search terms in your cloud workspace or the shared board cache. Ordinary hosting request logs can still contain the request URL and network information. Opening an employer’s posting contacts that employer or recruitment provider directly.

What the service receives

The hosted service supports ChatGPT sign-in and, when the service owner has completed the relevant configuration, direct Google and LinkedIn sign-in. Only available sign-in methods are offered. You may also choose Google on the ChatGPT sign-in screen. Career Finder does not receive your provider passwords. Signing in supplies a stable provider account identifier and your account email, with an optional display name. Direct Google and LinkedIn sign-in require an email address that the provider reports as verified. Career Finder does not independently verify current mailbox ownership. Direct provider flows use short-lived authorization exchanges and protected browser sessions. These associate your private workspace with your account and support recovery on another phone. To add another provider to an existing workspace, sign in to that workspace first and connect the provider from Account or Settings. Matching email addresses alone never merge workspaces. This service does not request access to your ChatGPT conversations or connected apps. Automatic applications must use the email address supplied by the signed-in account. This helps prevent one account from submitting as another email address; it does not verify legal identity or job qualifications.

When you save from a connected phone, the service stores the profile fields, contact details, job preferences, saved opportunities, CV files, and employer answers that you provide. It also stores profile revisions, connection expiry information, application reviews, approvals, and submission outcomes. Immutable application snapshots preserve the exact information approved for a request.

LinkedIn connections, links, and searches

When configured by the service owner, direct LinkedIn sign-in requests the OpenID Connect scopes openid profile email. It stores the member identifier, an optional display name, and the verified email used for the Career Finder account. LinkedIn may omit an email or its verification status. If necessary, Career Finder makes one short-lived request to LinkedIn’s UserInfo endpoint and accepts its email only when the member identifier matches the verified sign-in response. If a verified email is still unavailable, Career Finder creates no new account or sign-in session from that attempt. You can instead use ChatGPT or Google to sign in and connect a basic LinkedIn profile to that workspace.

Connecting LinkedIn from an already signed-in Account or Settings page requests only openid profile. This basic connection does not request or collect a new LinkedIn email address. A verified email previously obtained by direct sign-in with the same LinkedIn identity can remain attached to that identity. Neither flow imports a CV, grants access to LinkedIn job-application APIs, verifies legal identity, or submits an application. A linked LinkedIn identity can recover the same workspace through direct sign-in only when LinkedIn supplies a verified email for that sign-in. You can disconnect an additional provider from Account or Settings. An original or current sign-in method must stay connected to preserve access; deleting the Career Finder account remains a separate choice. Saving a personal profile URL is also a separate choice.

You may save an optional personal LinkedIn profile URL. The app removes query parameters and fragments before saving it. Saving a URL does not verify ownership, connect a LinkedIn account, or import LinkedIn profile data. A connected phone uploads the link only when you save your profile. The exact link is shown in an application review before an authorized employer integration can receive it. Earlier application snapshots retain the link approved at that time.

Search LinkedIn opens LinkedIn directly with only the keywords and location you entered in the job search. It does not attach your Career Finder profile, CV, email, or session token. LinkedIn controls its own sign-in, search, application, and privacy practices. Career Finder does not scrape LinkedIn or submit LinkedIn applications automatically.

Where it is stored

The service is hosted through ChatGPT Sites using Cloudflare Workers, a D1 database, and private R2 file storage. CV files are accessed through authenticated workspace routes. They are not published as public file links. HTTPS protects transport; the phone stores its connection token in protected device storage. No system can promise absolute security.

When information is sent to an employer

Auto Apply requires a supported, authorized employer integration and your explicit approval of a prepared review. The request sends the reviewed profile, exact CV, message, and applicable consent answers to that employer’s applicant-tracking provider. The current integration supports configured Lever postings. Opening a job link or preparing a review does not submit an application.

Employer sites and their providers have their own privacy policies and retention rules. A confirmed receipt means the supported provider returned acceptance and an application ID. Uncertain results stay marked uncertain and are blocked from automatic resend.

Your controls

  • Review and update your profile before saving.
  • Decide separately about each employer’s data-processing and future-contact questions.
  • Use Settings → Your saved data in the web app to export or clear that workspace’s saved browser data. Earlier drafts belonging to the same signed-in account can be exported or erased separately. Private application records and unfinished recovery copies have their own deletion controls.
  • Use Account to manage available sign-in connections, revoke phone connections, and export your online records. The phone web app also offers provider connection controls in Settings.
  • Download current or retained CV files through authenticated links, and remove unused versions that are not required by your profile or active application reviews.
  • Delete the active cloud workspace and its CV files, or delete connection metadata for an account without a workspace, from the Account page.

Retention and deletion

Saved profiles, opportunities, and application records remain in your workspace until you delete it, subject to service storage limits. Unused uploaded CV versions can be removed separately; the service checks current references before erasure. Up to five PDF versions can be retained, each up to 5 MiB, within a 25 MiB account limit. Removing the current CV from a profile does not by itself erase a previously uploaded version. Remove an eligible unused version from Account to reclaim its storage. Deletion from Account removes active workspace records and CV contents, revokes phone connections, and removes provider identity links, browser sessions, and pending account-bound sign-in requests. Accounts without a workspace can also export and delete their connection metadata. Provider access and refresh tokens are not retained. Google or LinkedIn may keep their own permission grant until you revoke it in that provider’s settings. A hashed deletion marker prevents an already pending sign-in from recreating a deleted link during its ten-minute authorization window. The marker becomes inactive after that window and is erased during later sign-in or deletion housekeeping. A separate durable owner marker contains only a private keyed hash, a random generation, and a deletion time. It prevents older requests from recreating deleted records; it contains no plaintext account identifier, email, profile, CV, or session credential. A provider request already in progress can temporarily block deletion. Interrupted deletion remains pending until removal is confirmed.

After a file is erased or an upload is retired, the service keeps an empty object at a random storage key so a delayed upload cannot restore deleted content. These empty markers contain no CV bytes, account identifier, filename, or document fingerprint.

Deleting a workspace cannot recall an application already sent to an employer or erase a file already saved on your phone. Hosting providers may retain infrastructure logs or recovery copies under their own service policies; this preview does not promise immediate removal from infrastructure backups or a separately operated backup-restoration service.

Service logs and tracking

The service uses bounded security counters and operational information to protect pairing and prevent repeated submissions. After clearing a browser draft, a small workspace version marker may remain to prevent an older open tab from restoring deleted content. It contains no profile, CV, notes, or encryption key. A separate encrypted Stop lock can also retain the workspace and review identifiers, request time, and whether Stop was confirmed. It contains no profile, CV, or private notes and prevents clearing personal drafts from reviving an earlier application approval. Hosting providers may process network and request metadata to operate their services. This deployment does not add advertising, third-party analytics, or use CV and application content for ad targeting. Application request bodies, tokens, and CV contents should not be included in issue reports or application logs.

Questions and updates

Check Help and support for technical guidance and the project issue route. Public issues are unsuitable for private information. This notice will be updated when the implemented data flows change.

These public pages and support requests

These help and privacy pages are hosted on Cloudflare Workers and can be read without signing into Career Finder. They add no sign-in form, analytics script, advertising, or embedded external media. Cloudflare may process technical request information, including IP addresses and traffic data, to provide and secure its services. Read the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

The Career Finder issue tracker is public. Posting requires a GitHub account, and your username, request and attachments can be seen by the maintainer and other people. Content may remain public or be copied by others. Do not include your CV, email address, connection code, session token, private application notes or an unredacted export. This channel does not guarantee a response time, confidentiality or removal. Use GitHub’s available editing and privacy controls for your public posts.
